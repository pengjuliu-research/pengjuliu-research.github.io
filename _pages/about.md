Hi, I'm **Pengju Liu** (刘鹏举), a PhD student working at the intersection of **building energy systems** and **human-centered built environment design**.

My current research focuses on **demand-response-oriented building energy systems** — exploring how buildings can act as flexible energy assets in grids dominated by renewable generation, while still delivering comfortable and healthy indoor environments for the people inside them. I'm particularly interested in physics-informed reduced-order modeling (RC models), data-driven control strategies, and the trade-offs between grid services and occupant well-being.

I'm building this site to share my work in progress, publications, and notes. Thanks for stopping by — feel free to reach out at the email on the right.
